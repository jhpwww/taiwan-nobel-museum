Nobel Prize in Physics, 2018

Prof. Donna Strickland

During the pandemic, science revised itself in public.

Why trust in science is important

Date
12 January 2026
Venue
National Taiwan University
Affiliation
University of Waterloo, Canada
  • Applied physics
  • Science & responsibility
  • How discovery happens

Guide video

GPS, the internet and the personal computer all came out of science that took years to get right. Strickland's point is that revising in public is not a failure of science but its ordinary method, this time carried out in front of everyone.

Two versions, side by side

Each laureate spoke twice: the formal lecture in Stockholm in the year of the award, and this one in Taiwan. Years apart, before a different audience, the account is not always the same. The pair repays watching together.
