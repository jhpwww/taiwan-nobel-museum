Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences, 2007

Prof. Eric S. Maskin

As the economy grew, so did the gap.

Globalization now takes the form of global value chains: one product, many tasks, many skill levels. High-skill work connects to the world market; low-skill work does not. Maskin's remedy lies not in less trade but in equipping more people to take part in it.
