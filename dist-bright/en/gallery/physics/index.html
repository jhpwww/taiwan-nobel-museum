About this prize

The Nobel Prize in Physics recognises the discovery or invention of greatest benefit to humankind. The list opens with X-rays and radioactivity, passes through quantum mechanics, and reaches the accelerating universe and gravitational waves. A century of physics is more or less contained in it.

First awarded in 1901 to Wilhelm Röntgen, for the discovery of X-rays. It has since been withheld 6 times, mostly during the two world wars.
