Nobel Lecture Museum

Learning area

A few tools for visitors who want to go further: gather what a lecture argued, set down your own response, and draft the question you would ask.

Three stages of watching a lecture

It applies to any lecture in the collection.

  1. 1 Prepare The guide video, the original Nobel Lecture, the official citation, and a question to carry in.
  2. 2 Attend Listen, ask, discuss.
  3. 3 Reflect A summary of the argument, a personal response, and at least one further question.

Used by a course

An NTU general-education course, Into the Nobel, is built around this collection. Students on it can use the tools above; everything here is open to every visitor and nothing requires enrolment.

How the course is assessed
  • 50% Attendance Both campus lectures are compulsory; missing either is a fail. Other sessions cost 10 points each. Outside sessions add 5 points each, up to 10.
  • 20% Group report Oral reports before and after each lecture.
  • 10% Question competition Submit a deep, logical, original question before the lecture; judged by experts. Late: −3, −6, −9 on days one to three.
  • 10% Viewing notes Choose 6 Taiwan Bridges videos, watch each with its original Nobel Lecture, and submit a note for each (summary / reflection / question). Same late penalty.
  • 10% Final report Due 09:00 Monday of week 17 via Google Form. Late means zero.
