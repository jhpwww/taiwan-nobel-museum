About this site

The Taiwan Bridges Program is run jointly by National Taiwan University and Academia Sinica, with President Ching-te Lai as honorary chair, initiated by Academician Yuan-Tseh Lee and supported by Raymond Soong, founder of Lite-On Technology. Across 12 Taiwanese research and teaching institutions and in partnership with the International Peace Foundation, it brought 31 Nobel laureates to Taiwan to lecture between 2025 and 2026.

Six of those lectures were hosted by NTU under the Raymond Soong Chair Professorship of Distinguished Research. The lectures, guide videos and interviews were scattered across several channels and institutional pages. This site gathers them into one place.
